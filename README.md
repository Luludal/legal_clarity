**Legal Clarity** is a specialized, modern legal web application designed for Kenya. It provides dual dedicated portals for individual clients and licensed legal advocates.

---

## 🏛️ Platform Overview

### 1. For Individuals (Clients)
Individual client access is focused purely on:
- **AI Contract Review & Loophole Detection**: Upload agreements, leases, employment contracts, or NDAs. The AI diagnostic scanner flags ambiguous notice periods, infinite liability exposure, missing Kenyan governing laws, and generates a risk index.
- **Direct Lawyer Consultation**: Connect directly with verified Law Society of Kenya (LSK) advocates to seek formal advice and representation.
- **Statutory Court Fee & Timeline Estimator**: Transparent estimations for magistrate vs. high court filing fees and dispute durations.

### 2. For Lawyers (Advocates)
A comprehensive practice suite for licensed advocates:
- **Encrypted Pleadings Vault**: 256-bit AES encrypted storage for plaints, witness affidavits, and documentary evidence compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.
- **Case Docket & Hearing Diaries**: Track court numbers, cause lists, and hearing schedules.
- **Client Messaging Channel**: Confidential advocate-client communication protected by legal professional privilege.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Constitution of Kenya 2010 Indexed**: Direct citations covering the Bill of Rights (Articles 19–59), Land & Property (Art. 40), and Judicial Authority (Art. 159).
- **Instant Light / Dark Mode**: Seamless visual toggle with instantaneous real-time rendering.
- **Subtle Scales of Justice Emblem**: Professional, clean branding with zero bloat.
- **Single Page App Routing**: Full client-side routing on Vercel with zero 404 errors.
